Add Coke sales & profitability analysis workbook

Add Coke-sales-analysis-implementation.xlsx, a formula-driven analysis
built on the 2021 retailer sales data (3,888 rows).

- Sales_Profit: units, revenue, profit, margin by retailer and region
- Price_Analysis: price vs volume/revenue/margin by brand and price band,
  with correlations
- Location_Margin: all 50 states ranked by operating margin, with Top 10
  and Bottom 10 views
- Monthly_Trends: monthly/quarterly trends, seasonality index, MoM change,
  brand-by-month heatmap
- Heatmaps: Region x Month, Region x Brand, Retailer x Region, and margin
- Explorer: drop-down filters (Retailer/Region/Brand) driving a monthly
  table and chart
- Pivot_Guide: steps for building native PivotTables and slicers in Excel
- Bar and line charts throughout; all figures are live formulas on Data
